8-Week transition plan
Week 1
Moisturizer testing begins
  • Begin testing Belif True Cream, Byoma Gel-Cream, Byoma Milky
  • Retire Belif Frozen Cream
  • Continue all other current products unchanged
  • 9Wishes still in PM until empty
👀 Watch: oiliness, dryness, or congestion from new moisturizers
Week 2
Toner + barrier serum swap
  • Belif Milky Toner → ✈️ travel kit
  • Good Molecules Niacinamide Toner → AM + PM
  • HA Serum → ✈️ travel kit
  • YTTP Skin Drip → AM only first
  • Continue: 9Wishes · Azelaic Acid · Naturium · Reedle 300
👀 Watch: congestion from Skin Drip — patch test jaw first
Week 3
Clarins in when Naturium finishes
  • Naturium runs out → Clarins Double Serum PM
  • Continue: 9Wishes · Azelaic Acid · Reedle 300
👀 Watch: seamless swap — same step, very low risk
Week 4
9Wishes finishes
  • 9Wishes runs out → YTTP Kale or Dokdo Cleanser PM only
  • Continue: Azelaic Acid · Reedle 300
👀 Watch: low risk — simple cleanser consolidation
Week 5
Reedle 300 finishes + Topicals Faded arrives
  • Reedle 300 runs out → Reedle 100 nightly
  • Topicals Faded arrives → Azelaic to ✈️ travel kit
  • Faded starts 3x/week AM — Mon · Wed · Fri
👀 Watch: mild tingling = normal · burning = pull back to 2x/week
Week 6
Faded daily + eye products
  • Topicals Faded → daily AM
  • The Ordinary Caffeine + EGCG → AM + PM
👀 Watch: milia under eye — if occurs, use AM only
Week 7
Glycolic toner once
  • Glycolic 7% → one Wednesday PM only
  • Skip tretinoin entirely that night
  • No Reedle Shot on glycolic nights
👀 Watch: redness next day = wait another week before trying again
Week 8
Full routine locked in 🎉
  • Second glycolic night if Week 7 went well
  • Spa day routine begins
  • Full routine completely established
